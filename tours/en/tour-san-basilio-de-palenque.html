San Basilio de Palenque Tour: First Free Town in America

Travel to the first free African settlement in the Americas, declared Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity by UNESCO.

Home > Tours > San Basilio de Palenque Tour

The San Basilio de Palenque Tour is an authentic day trip from Cartagena (approx. 1 hour 15 mins drive). Led by native Palenquero guides, you will experience their unique Creole language (Bantu-Spanish), traditional drum workshops, medicinal herbalist wisdom, and an authentic Afro-Colombian lunch.

Tour Details & Overview

Duration 6 hours (approx. 8:00 AM to 2:00 PM)
Transportation Round-trip air-conditioned van from Cartagena
Includes Local guide, drum workshop, traditional lunch & tastings
Languages English and Spanish
Location San Basilio de Palenque, Mahates, Bolívar

Living Memory of African Freedom in the Americas

Founded by maroon leader Benkos Biohó in the early 1600s, Palenque successfully resisted the Spanish Crown to gain sovereign royal freedom in 1691—over a century before the Americas declared independence. Visiting Palenque supports the community directly and preserves ancient oral and musical traditions.
