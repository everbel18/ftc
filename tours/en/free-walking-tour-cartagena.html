Why Take This Walking Tour on Your Very First Day?

Cartagena de Indias is a UNESCO World Heritage jewel packed with fascinating pirate sieges, Spanish military architecture, and vibrant Afro-Caribbean heritage. Starting your trip with our tour gives you an immediate mental map of the city, trusted restaurant recommendations where locals actually dine, crucial street safety tips, and the historical context behind every stone rampart and blooming bougainvillea balcony.

Unlike crowded commercial bus groups, Free Tour Cartagena keeps walking groups manageable so you can clearly hear your guide, ask personalized questions, and capture amazing photos in the most photogenic colonial corners.

Step-by-Step Itinerary

Explore our detailed stops to make the absolute most of your time in Cartagena:

1. Plaza Santa Teresa (Starting Point)

Welcome under the yellow umbrella by the Naval Museum with an introduction to Cartagena's 1533 founding.

2. Plaza San Pedro Claver

Admire the colonial sanctuary of San Pedro Claver, Caribbean human rights history, and unique iron sculptures.

3. Customs Square (Plaza de la Aduana)

The city's oldest commercial plaza, former colonial counting house, and residence of founder Pedro de Heredia.

4. Clock Tower & Portal de los Dulces

The magnificent monumental gateway to the fortress city and traditional sweets made by Afro-descendant Palenqueras.

5. Proclamation Square & Bolívar Park

Historic site of the 1811 Declaration of Absolute Independence and shady park flanked by the Palace of Inquisition.

6. Santo Domingo Square

Fernando Botero's famous bronze sculpture "Gertrudis" and colonial legends of Cartagena's oldest church.

7. City Walls & Fortresses

Scenic walk atop America's most formidable military fortifications overlooking the Caribbean Sea.

8. Getsemaní (Plaza de la Trinidad)

Transition into the bohemian neighborhood: vibrant graffiti murals, hanging umbrella streets, and lively local culture.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Cartagena free walking tour really free?

Yes, there is no mandatory upfront fee. It operates on a pay-what-you-wish voluntary tip model based on your satisfaction.

How much tip is customary?

Tips are voluntary. Most happy travelers give between $30,000 and $50,000 COP ($8 to $15 USD) per person.

Where does the tour meet and how do I spot the guide?

We meet at Plaza Santa Teresa outside the Caribbean Naval Museum. Look for our guides holding bright yellow umbrellas.

What time does the walking tour start?

Departures run daily at 9:30 AM, 10:00 AM, and 4:00 PM. Please arrive 10-15 minutes prior to departure.

What languages are available?

Tours are conducted simultaneously in separate English and Spanish groups with dedicated guides.

Do I need to reserve in advance?

Yes, online booking is free and strongly recommended to guarantee your spot within our limited group sizes.

What happens if it rains?

Cartagena tropical showers are usually brief. Tours depart as scheduled; guides adapt the route to covered colonial arcades.

Can I cancel or modify my reservation?

Yes, you can easily cancel or change your reservation at any time free of charge using the link in your confirmation email.

Is the tour suitable for kids and families?

Absolutely! Our storytelling is engaging and fun for travelers of all ages, with easy flat walking routes.

Is the tour wheelchair and stroller accessible?

Yes, the route follows flat streets. While some colonial curbs are uneven, our guides assist and maintain a comfortable pace.

Where does the tour end?

It finishes either at Plaza Santo Domingo or Plaza de la Trinidad in Getsemaní, surrounded by great dining and easy taxi access.

What should I bring on the tour?

Comfortable walking shoes, light breathable clothing, sunscreen, sunglasses, a hat, and cold bottled water.

Do you offer tours for cruise ship passengers?

Yes, we provide dedicated 4-hour shore excursions with port pickup in Manga, private air conditioning, and guaranteed return.

What if my cruise ship docks late?

We actively track ship arrival schedules at Manga port and adjust tour departure times without any penalty.

Do you offer private VIP tours?

Yes! All our walking and cultural tours are available as private experiences with custom departure times.
