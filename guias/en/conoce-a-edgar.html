Tours Led Personally by Edgar in Cartagena

Since his school days—when his favorite classes were History, Geography, and English—Edgar has dedicated his life to showcasing the authentic Cartagena. Today, he personally leads these signature experiences:

100% in English

Old City Free Walking Tour

Edgar personally leads all English-language walking tours through the Historic Center and Walled City, connecting international travelers with colonial history in an engaging, rigorous, and entertaining way.

Local Immersion

Bazurto Market Tour

His signature culinary and cultural immersion into the vibrant labyrinth of Bazurto Market: exotic fruit tastings, traditional Colombian kitchens, champeta music, and genuine connection with locals.

Tailored · Bocagrande

Private Tours & Patient Pickups

Customized private tours tailored to each party with convenient pickup directly at hotels, including dedicated assistance picking up visitors and medical recovery guests staying in Bocagrande, Castillogrande, or the Old City.

African Heritage

San Basilio de Palenque Excursion

An inspiring historical and cultural journey to the first free town in the Americas (UNESCO World Heritage Site), guided with deep respect for its roots, Palenquero language, drumming traditions, and gastronomy.
