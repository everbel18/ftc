What to Eat in Cartagena: Iconic Caribbean Flavors

A mouthwatering guide to the most authentic dishes, street snacks, and culinary heritage in Cartagena de Indias.

Home > Travel Guides > What to Eat in Cartagena

The must-try dishes in Cartagena include: Arepa de Huevo (crisp corn dough filled with seasoned meat and a fried egg), Arroz con Coco (sweet coconut rice), Pescado Frito (fresh whole fried snapper with patacones), Ceviche de Camarón with lime and pink sauce, and tropical sweets prepared by Palenqueras.

Tour Details & Overview

The Fusion of African, Indigenous & Spanish Flavors

Cartagena's coastal culinary traditions reflect centuries of blending African frying techniques, indigenous cassava roots, and Spanish spices. From street vendors around Plaza de la Trinidad to fine colonial dining courtyards, the city is a paradise for adventurous foodies.
