Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Cartagena free walking tour really free?

Yes, joining the tour has no fixed price or entry fee. It operates on a pay-what-you-wish voluntary gratuity model.

How much tip is recommended?

Tipping is voluntary. Most travelers contribute between $30,000 and $50,000 COP ($8 to $15 USD) per person.

Where does the tour start and how do I spot the guide?

We meet at Plaza Santa Teresa outside the Naval Museum. Look for our guides holding bright yellow umbrellas.

What time does the tour begin?

Daily departures run at 9:30 AM, 10:00 AM, and 4:00 PM. Please arrive 10-15 minutes prior.

In what languages is the tour offered?

Tours are conducted simultaneously in separate English and Spanish groups.

Do I need to reserve in advance?

Yes, reserving online is free and recommended to ensure your spot within our limited groups.

What happens if it rains?

Cartagena tropical showers pass quickly. The tour departs rain or shine, adapting to covered colonial walkways.

Can I cancel or change my reservation?

Yes, you can easily change or cancel your reservation anytime via your confirmation email link.

Are children allowed to join?

Yes, children of all ages are welcome. Our storytelling is dynamic and family-friendly.

Is the tour accessible for persons with reduced mobility?

Yes, the route is predominantly flat. Guides adapt pace to assist wheelchairs and baby strollers.

Where does the walking tour finish?

The historic tour finishes at Plaza de la Merced, and the combined tour finishes at Avenida del Pedregal in Getsemaní, both locations with great dining and easy transportation access.

What should I bring for the tour?

Light comfortable clothes, walking sneakers, hat, sunglasses, sunscreen, and water.

Do you offer tours for cruise passengers?

Yes, we provide 4-hour shore excursions with port pickup in Manga and guaranteed return.

What if my cruise ship arrives late?

We track port arrival times and adjust pickup schedules without any fee.

Do you offer private tours?

Yes, private VIP tours are available for families and couples with flexible schedules.
