Literary & Cultural Tour · Gabriel García Márquez

Ruta Macondo

A journey through Cartagena's Historic and Cultural Center across the places where Nobel Laureate Gabriel García Márquez lived, worked, and found inspiration.

Home > Travel Guides > Ruta Macondo

Our RUTA MACONDO tour in the Historic and Cultural Center of Cartagena is a circuit filled with anecdotes, lesser-known stories, and the places where Colombian Nobel Laureate in Literature Gabriel García Márquez worked, lived, and found inspiration for many of his works during his stay and intimate relationship with this city, which also holds his final resting place.

About the Tour

We walk through the charming narrow streets and plazas of beautiful Cartagena in approximately 2 hours and 50 minutes, making 10 important stops where around 26 iconic places are located—sites that inspired the magical realism of the author of "One Hundred Years of Solitude", "Love in the Time of Cholera", "The Story of a Shipwrecked Sailor", and "Of Love and Other Demons".

The exact route order will depend on the starting point arranged for your group.

Ruta Macondo: Discover the literary Cartagena and magical realism of Gabriel García Márquez.

Landmarks & Stations of the Ruta Macondo

Along our circuit through Cartagena's Historic and Cultural Center, we will visit the following iconic sites (the order of the tour will depend on the starting point):

1. Hotel Santa Teresa

2. Baluarte de San Francisco Javier (San Francisco Javier Bastion)

3. Cartagena International Film Festival Headquarters (FICCI)

4. Naval Museum (Museo Naval)

5. Calle San Juan de Dios – El Universal Newspaper

6. Gabriel García Márquez Foundation for New Ibero-American Journalism

7. Plaza San Pedro – San Pedro Claver Church

8. Museum of Modern Art of Cartagena “MAMC”

9. Plaza de los Coches

10. Clock Tower Gate (Puerta o Torre del Reloj)

11. Calle del Arzobispado

12. Heladería Americana

13. Hotel Monserrate
